Scribe is a lightweight and fast, cross platform email program. Features an easy to use interface, split view of folders and items, handles attachments, signatures, drag and drop and has an address book and calendar. No install/uninstall is necessary as all settings and libraries are stored in the main directory. Supports bayesian spam filtering. Includes lots of tools for importing and exporting data in standard and popular formats. And has a range of plugins to enhance the function of the program.
